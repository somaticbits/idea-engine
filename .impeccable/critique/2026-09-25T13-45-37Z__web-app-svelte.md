# Design Specificity Verdict
The kinetic branch map, route history and progression from seed to pinned concept make this feel authored for Idea Engine. Detector scan was clean (`[]` for `web/App.svelte` and `web/ThreadCanvas.svelte`). Live inspection at 390×844 and 1440×900 found the mobile path rail clipped horizontally, map hints/controls crowding the top of the canvas, and branch labels near that control band. Nearby-choice rail and pinned-concept dock preserve important actions. Desktop focused map has substantial open canvas around a small set of nodes; inspected trip showed 93 hidden ideas. Saved trips and an existing trip were exercised. First-run API-key setup, empty states and paid-call failure states were reviewed in source, not triggered. Accessibility snapshot flagged seed/settings controls as missing accessible names, but source has associated label-for/id pairs; treat as inconclusive, not a confirmed markup defect.

# Overall Impression
The creative premise and end-to-end path are strong. The core opportunity is to retain exploration's calm while making graph scale, mobile wayfinding and spend context more legible.

# What's Working
- The metaphor carries end-to-end: seed flow, branch map, route history and concept card form a coherent journey.
- Context stays close to the work: current idea, traveled path, nearby ideas, pinned state and budgeted spend appear in the trip experience.
- Consequential actions have safeguards: deletion confirmation, generation/retry feedback and copy recovery support recovery.

# Priority Issues
1. **[P1] The core graph needs stronger wayfinding as it grows.** Full-graph mode can expose many branches at once, while current/visited/pinned/cross-link states are conveyed mostly through position, lines and color. Pin actions are visually hidden until hover/focus on fine-pointer devices. Fix: add a compact legend, keep pin actions discoverable, make nearby/list view a strong non-spatial alternative, and explain when zoom prevents selection. Suggested: `/impeccable clarify`.
2. **[P1] Put spend context where users decide to begin.** Setup discloses that calls are charged, while budgeted spend is shown in-trip and limits live in Settings. Fix: surface configured limits and a concise explanation of budget reservations beside start/expand actions. Suggested: `/impeccable onboard`.
3. **[P1] Mobile trip wayfinding is crowded at the canvas edge.** At 390px, the horizontal path rail clips long ancestry, and the map hint/control band competes with branch labels near the top. Fix: reserve clear zones for path, controls and nodes; make current path easier to scan. Suggested: `/impeccable adapt`.
4. **[P2] Large graph and long-trip scale needs a calmer overview.** The inspected trip had 109 ideas and 93 hidden from focused view; revealing all at once risks a label cloud. Fix: group by depth/parent, provide search/filtering, preserve focused-path overview as default. Suggested: `/impeccable distill`.
5. **[P2] Settings and technical terms need more progressive disclosure.** Kit, credentials, limits and destructive deletion share one long surface; “OpenRouter,” “agent prompt,” and budget reservations are not equally accessible to first-time creative users. Fix: separate destructive/account actions, explain provider terminology at point of use, and add short export format guidance. Suggested: `/impeccable clarify`.

# Persona Red Flags

- **Sam, accessibility-dependent user:** Keyboard-focusable idea buttons exist, but no evident spatial keyboard-navigation model or map-state legend. Visible labels appear associated in source despite snapshot flag; validate with a screen reader first.
- **Casey, mobile user:** Nearby rail/dock help, but clipped path and crowded map-control band hinder wayfinding and branch inspection.
