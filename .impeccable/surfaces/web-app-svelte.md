# Focused branch explorer

Mode: Operate. Desktop-first creative sessions; a compact phone flow remains fully usable. The user chooses a thread, compares nearby associations and pins a buildable idea. Preserve trips, model calls, cost controls and exports. No generated words enter the visual design as executable markup.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Exploration feels like handling a kinetic mobile: steady support, branching weights, one gesture of attention. Refuse the all-at-once force cloud and the settings-heavy dashboard.

**OWN-WORLD:** Frosted celadon studio ground, carbon ink structure, copper active weight and mineral green pinned weight. Thin tension rods and suspended enamel discs are functional graph grammar, not decorative stars. Bricolage Grotesque titles; Atkinson Hyperlegible controls and copy.

**STORY:** The user sees their path and current center. Immediate branches orbit around it. Tapping one brings it to the center; its own branches form the next orbit. An ancestor remains one click away.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Desktop: spare top rail, prominent path, large radial map, right named-branch list and current-center actions. Phone: path, compact radial map, tappable branch list and an action tray immediately below.

**FORM:** Kinetic sculpture mobile, assigned grounded form 5, seed 029c5e39. The current node anchors a radial orbit; tapping a branch moves it into the center with a short landing animation, then lays out its children around it. Numbers link physical weights to accessible names. A mobile list is the complete fallback.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
